## Verdict in one paragraph

The engineering is strong. The Convex judge called the backend "the strongest Convex code I'd expect at a hackathon": no `.collect()`, every query on an index, one atomic, idempotent mutation per incident. The risk now is **the stage, not the code**:
1. Anyone on the internet can wipe or fire the live demo.
2. The script's numbers contradict what the screen shows.
3. The demo has too many beats for 3 minutes.
4. A full UI rewrite is landing hours before freeze.
5. One finance hole the Hyphenate judge will find: after the call, the controller can only pay the **changed** account or reject.

## (e) Strongest dissent

1. **"Stop the remake" vs "the remake is the fix."**
   - The ponytail, UX, general-judge and founder voices: a full UI rewrite hours before freeze, with no UI tests, is the biggest self-inflicted risk. A working ugly demo beats a beautiful broken one.
   - On the other side, the remake prompt itself (§1.3) and the general judge's own "polish 6/10, a column of identical grey cards" both say the current PE view loses the story in the first 10 seconds.
   - Synthesis: the **thin stage pass** delivers most of the judge-visible gain (order, hero card, panel dock, incident pin) at a fraction of the risk. Record the backup video from main first. Omar decides.
2. **The map isn't the product** (PE buyer, founder). "The hold plus a record that each company acted is the product; the map is the demo." Spend freeze hours on the money moment, not on map particles.
3. **The premise skeptic.** No public case of one shared vendor hitting several companies of one PE firm exists (and we must never claim one). The webhook source is hand-waved. The planted scorecard proves restraint, not accuracy. All three have honest rebuttals (section (b)). They must be *said*, not discovered by a judge.
4. **The PE buyer on the Bain framing.** A firm-wide map full of unacknowledged alerts is discoverable proof that the firm *knew*. Pitch "a record that each company acted". Acknowledgements, controls log and containment are already that record.
5. **The Convex judge: don't touch `convex/`** except the reset lock and maybe `returns:`. The architecture already impresses; every deploy now is risk.
