## Priority issues (by demo impact)

1. **[P0] The $480K doesn't read from the back of the room.**
   - The amount is `text-2xl` (`PaymentCard.tsx:88`).
   - It sits in a 2-column grid even when there is only one card (`HeldPaymentsPanel.tsx:30`), under Alerts.
   - At 1440×900, Release is below the fold.
   - Fix:
     - Put an escalated hold first and full-width, above Alerts (`CompanyView.tsx`).
     - Make the amount `text-4xl` / `text-5xl`.
     - Use one column when there is one card.
2. **[P0] The held card repeats itself and pushes Release below the fold.**
   - Fix:
     - Keep the amount, the bank-change block and one line on what to do.
     - Move "Why it's held" and `row.notice` into the existing `<details>`.
     - Turn the escalation banner into a "Decision needed · due in ~20h" chip.
3. **[P1] Release is one click; Reject asks for confirmation.**
   - `PaymentCard.tsx:57-64` confirms only `reject`, yet releasing to ••7731 is the risky action.
   - Fix: confirm Release and name the destination: "Release $480,000 to ••7731 (changed by email 2 days before the incident)?"
4. **[P1] Instructions are shown to the wrong role.**
   - "Verify by calling…" renders whenever the payment is held (`isHeld`), not only when the viewer can decide (`canDecide`) (`PaymentCard.tsx:163`), so the PE partner sees it.
   - Rosa sees "Rosa Alvarez: call…", her own name in the third person.
   - Fix:
     - Show it only when the viewer can decide (gate on `canDecide`).
     - The PE view says "Waiting on Summit's controller".
     - Address the controller as "you".
5. **[P1] The map tile contradicts the pitch.**
   - Summit shows "same vendor only · score 70" next to "urgent", with no reason given.
   - The alerts for companies that only share the vendor say "Rotate any credentials you share with Brightline".
   - This undercuts demo beat 5 ("Same vendor isn't the same weakness").
   - Fix: show the deciding reason on the tile and hide the raw score.
   - Owners: Role 3 (`src/map/`) and Role 4 (alert text).

## Detector (browser overlay)

- **Counts:** 20 findings on the PE view, 7 on the company view. The source scan found 0.
- **Line length (11), real:** alert prose, the vendor letter and outbox rows run 120–152 characters per line, with no width cap.
- **Nested cards (13), mixed:**
  - Payment cards inside panels: real.
  - Coloured strips and the Proof stat tiles: false positives.
- **Stragglers (3), cosmetic:** the masked `••NNNN` wraps onto a line on its own.

## Minor

- Amber is overused: the header Fire incident button, the timeline sentence and every tile's "$X held".
- Switching user flashes the sign-in screen and "Loading…".
- On 375px the PE headline breaks at "·".
- The $92,000 card's chip wraps.
- Tokens drift from the brand:
  - the `danger` / `ok` / `panel` names;
  - 6px corners;
  - `brand/tokens.css` isn't imported.
- The PE headline and tile amounts don't use `tabular-nums`.
- The disabled Release button has no `aria-describedby`.
- Screen readers read "••" and "→" aloud as symbols.
