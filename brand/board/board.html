The brief

A cross-company security alert system for private equity portfolios. When one portfolio company is breached, Sidecall finds which sister companies share the same attack surface (a shared login, MSP, payroll provider or identity system) and warns them before the attacker moves sideways, without ever revealing which company was hit.

Success: reads as a warning system, not a weapon or a wall; avoids padlocks, shields, graphs, radar and the navy/electric-blue palette; holds at 16 px; reads on a dark projector slide.

Final design

Stopped Ripple

Four sibling tiles share one circular edge; the centre, the company that was hit, is never drawn.

  • One shared exposure reaches every sibling at once, shown as the same cut in each tile
  • The breached company is negative space, matching the promise to never reveal it
  • No shield, wall or crosshair: the ripple is an absence, not a blast
  • Amber on graphite: a calm heads-up, clear of the category's red and navy
